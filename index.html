📌 Idea / Concept: Identifying Enlargement vs. Reduction, Dilation Factor $k$ & Finding Unknowns

📌 Determining Enlargement/Reduction & Dilation Factor
1 In the given figure: Determine whether the dilation with center $C$ mapping $Q$ to $Q'$ is an enlargement or a reduction. Then find the dilation factor and the value of $x$.
✍️ Student Workspace (20 Lines)
✅ Model Step-by-Step Solution Question 1
  • Since $Q \in \overline{CQ'}$ (and $CQ' > CQ$), the dilation with center $C$ mapping $Q$ to $Q'$ is an enlargement.
  • Finding the dilation factor $k$:
    $$k = \dfrac{CQ'}{CQ} = \dfrac{15}{5} = 3$$
  • Finding the value of $x$:
    $$\dfrac{x}{4} = 3 \implies x = 4 \times 3 = 12$$
Enlargement  |  Dilation Factor $k = 3$  |  $x = 12$
📌 Finding Missing Side Lengths $x$ and $y$ Under Dilation with Center $C$
2 In the given figure: Dilation with center $C$ makes $\triangle J'K'M'$ the image of $\triangle JKM$. Find the dilation factor $k$, and then find the values of $x$ and $y$.
✍️ Student Workspace (20 Lines)
✅ Model Step-by-Step Solution Question 2
  • Since $\triangle J'K'M'$ is the image of $\triangle JKM$ under a dilation with center $C$:
  • 1) Finding the dilation factor $k$:
    $$k = \dfrac{K'M'}{KM} = \dfrac{28}{14} = 2$$
  • 2) Finding the values of $x$ and $y$:
    • $\dfrac{J'K'}{JK} = k \implies \dfrac{16}{x} = 2 \implies x = \dfrac{16}{2} = 8$
    • $\dfrac{J'M'}{JM} = k \implies \dfrac{32}{y} = 2 \implies y = \dfrac{32}{2} = 16$
$k = 2, \quad x = 8, \quad y = 16$
📌 Dilation of a Polygon With Interior Center $O$
3 In the given figure: Dilation with center $O$ makes polygon $A'B'C'D'$ the image of polygon $ABCD$. Find the dilation factor $k$ and the value of $x$.
✍️ Student Workspace (20 Lines)
✅ Model Step-by-Step Solution Question 3
  • Since polygon $A'B'C'D'$ is the image of polygon $ABCD$ under a dilation with center $O$:
  • 1) Finding the dilation factor $k$:
    Given $OA = 6\text{ cm}$ and $OA' = 9\text{ cm}$:
    $$k = \dfrac{OA'}{OA} = \dfrac{9}{6} = \dfrac{3}{2}$$
  • 2) Finding the length $x$ (where $D'C' = x\text{ cm}$ and $DC = 6\text{ cm}$):
    $$\dfrac{D'C'}{DC} = k \implies \dfrac{x}{6} = \dfrac{3}{2} \implies x = \dfrac{3 \times 6}{2} = 9\text{ cm}$$
$k = \dfrac{3}{2}, \quad x = 9\text{ cm}$

📌 Idea / Concept: Dilation of Triangles, Parallel Segments ($\overline{DE} \parallel \overline{BC}$) & Missing Side Lengths

📌 Dilation of a Triangle with Vertex Center $A$
5 In the given figure: $\triangle ADE$ is the image of $\triangle ABC$ under a dilation with center $A$. Find the dilation factor, prove that $\overline{DE} \parallel \overline{BC}$, and then find the length of $\overline{BC}$.
✍️ Student Workspace (20 Lines)
✅ Model Step-by-Step Solution Question 5
  • Since $\triangle ADE$ is the image of $\triangle ABC$ under a dilation with center $A$, point $D$ is the image of point $B$, and $AB = AD + DB = 9 + 6 = 15\text{ cm}$.
  • 1) Dilation factor $k$:
    $$k = \dfrac{AD}{AB} = \dfrac{9}{15} = \dfrac{3}{5}$$
  • 2) Proving $\overline{DE} \parallel \overline{BC}$:
    Since $\overline{DE}$ is the image of $\overline{BC}$ under a dilation, a line segment and its image under dilation are parallel $\implies \overline{DE} \parallel \overline{BC}$.
  • 3) Finding the length of $\overline{BC}$:
    $$\dfrac{DE}{BC} = k \implies \dfrac{7.5}{BC} = \dfrac{3}{5} \implies BC = \dfrac{5 \times 7.5}{3} = 12.5\text{ cm}$$
$k = \dfrac{3}{5}$  |  $\overline{DE} \parallel \overline{BC}$  |  $BC = 12.5\text{ cm}$
6 Self-Evaluation In the following figure: $\triangle ABC$ is the image of $\triangle ADE$ under a dilation with center $A$. Find the dilation factor, prove that $\overline{DE} \parallel \overline{BC}$, and then find the length of $\overline{AC}$.
✍️ Student Workspace (20 Lines)
✅ Model Step-by-Step Solution Question 6
  • Since $\triangle ABC$ is the image of $\triangle ADE$ under a dilation with center $A$, point $B$ is the image of point $D$, and $AB = AD + DB = 5.4 + 12.6 = 18\text{ cm}$.
  • 1) Dilation factor $k$:
    $$k = \dfrac{AB}{AD} = \dfrac{18}{5.4} = \dfrac{10}{3}$$
  • 2) Proving $\overline{DE} \parallel \overline{BC}$:
    Since $\overline{BC}$ is the image of $\overline{DE}$ under a dilation, $\overline{DE} \parallel \overline{BC}$.
  • 3) Finding the length of $\overline{AC}$:
    $$\dfrac{AC}{AE} = k \implies \dfrac{AC}{6} = \dfrac{10}{3} \implies AC = \dfrac{6 \times 10}{3} = 20\text{ cm}$$
$k = \dfrac{10}{3}$  |  $\overline{DE} \parallel \overline{BC}$  |  $AC = 20\text{ cm}$

📌 Idea / Concept: Dilation in the Coordinate Plane $(x, y) \longrightarrow (kx, ky)$

📌 Enlargement in the Coordinate Plane ($k = 2$)
7 Draw $\triangle ABC$ with vertices $A(1, 3)$, $B(2, -1)$, $C(5, 0)$ in the coordinate plane below, then draw its image under a dilation with center $O$ and dilation factor $2$.
✍️ Student Workspace (20 Lines)
✅ Model Step-by-Step Solution Question 7
  • Use the dilation rule in the coordinate plane with center $O(0, 0)$ and dilation factor $k = 2$:
  • $$(x, y) \longrightarrow (2x, 2y)$$
  • Finding the coordinates of the image vertices:
    • $A(1, 3) \longrightarrow A'(2 \times 1, 2 \times 3) = A'(2, 6)$
    • $B(2, -1) \longrightarrow B'(2 \times 2, 2 \times (-1)) = B'(4, -2)$
    • $C(5, 0) \longrightarrow C'(2 \times 5, 2 \times 0) = C'(10, 0)$
$A'(2, 6), \quad B'(4, -2), \quad C'(10, 0)$

📌 Finding Dilation Factor of Parallel Segments

📌 Dilation of a Polygon Sharing Vertex $B$

📌 Finding Dilation Factor From Point and Image Coordinates
15 Choose the correct answer from the given ones:
What ratio expresses the dilation factor of a dilation with center $O$ mapping the point $A(6, -2)$ to the point $B(3, -1)$?
✍️ Student Workspace (20 Lines)

📌 Inverse Dilation Factor (Mapping $A'$ Back to $A$)
16 Choose the correct answer from the given ones:
If $A'$ is the image of $A$ under a dilation with center at the origin and dilation factor $\dfrac{3}{4}$, what is the dilation factor that makes $A$ the image of $A'$ with center at the origin?
✍️ Student Workspace (20 Lines)
✅ Model Step-by-Step Solution Question 16
  • Since $OA' = \dfrac{3}{4} OA$, multiplying both sides by the multiplicative inverse $\dfrac{4}{3}$ gives:
  • $$OA = \dfrac{4}{3} OA'$$
  • Thus, the dilation factor that maps $A'$ back to $A$ is the reciprocal $\dfrac{4}{3}$.
Correct Choice: (b) $\dfrac{4}{3}$
📌 Finding the Original Pre-Image Point $A$ Given Its Image $A'$
17 Choose the correct answer from the given ones:
If $A'(12, -6)$ is the image of $A$ under a dilation with center at the origin and its rule is $(x, y) \longrightarrow \left(\dfrac{3}{2}x, \dfrac{3}{2}y\right)$, then the point $A$ is:
✍️ Student Workspace (20 Lines)
✅ Model Step-by-Step Solution Question 17
  • Let $A = (x, y)$. Then $\left(\dfrac{3}{2}x, \dfrac{3}{2}y\right) = (12, -6)$:
  • $$\dfrac{3}{2}x = 12 \implies x = 12 \times \dfrac{2}{3} = 8$$
  • $$\dfrac{3}{2}y = -6 \implies y = -6 \times \dfrac{2}{3} = -4$$
  • Therefore, the original point is $A(8, -4)$.
Correct Choice: (a) $(8, -4)$
📌 Condition for Reduction ($0 < k < 1$)
18 Choose the correct answer from the given ones:
Which of the following ratios represents a dilation factor for reduction of a figure?
✍️ Student Workspace (20 Lines)
✅ Model Step-by-Step Solution Question 18
  • A dilation is a reduction when its dilation factor $k$ satisfies $0 < k < 1$.
  • Comparing the given options:
    • $120\% = 1.2 > 1$ (enlargement)
    • $\dfrac{5}{4} = 1.25 > 1$ (enlargement)
    • $2 > 1$ (enlargement)
    • $50\% = \dfrac{50}{100} = \dfrac{1}{2} = 0.5 < 1$ (reduction ✓)
Correct Choice: (d) $50\%$
